How it works

Tap your strongest category – the higher value takes both cards. Lightning, knockouts and slow-motion finales make every comparison count.

  1. 1

    Pick

    Tap the strongest category on your top card.

  2. 2

    Compare

    Both cards go head to head with their values.

  3. 3

    CLASH!

    The higher value wins both cards – whoever ends up with all of them wins.

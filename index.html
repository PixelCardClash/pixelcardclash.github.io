    What awaits you

    Pick. Compare. CLASH!

    Tap your strongest category – the higher value takes both cards. Lightning, knockouts and slow-motion finales make every comparison count.

    Four rivals with personality

    From cheerful Kiki to ice-cold MAX-9000: every rival plays differently – and has something to say about it.

    Special rules shake things up

    Low wins, bonus system, One of Three, card thief, critical hit and more – switch them on individually and make every match different.

    Daily Duel & collection

    A new duel every day with the same cards for everyone. Earn up to three stars per card and track your records and form.

    Easy to learn

    A short practice duel with Kiki explains the basics; special rules are introduced the moment they first appear. Online duels with friends via Steam are available as an expansion (DLC).
